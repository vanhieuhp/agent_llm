# US Public EV Charging Landscape for Fleet Planning

## Public charging points in the US
The US has roughly **177,000 public EV charging ports** available, based on U.S. Department of Energy data from the Alternative Fueling Station Locator. That figure has continued to rise quickly as public infrastructure expands, but coverage remains uneven by region, with denser buildout in major metros and travel corridors.

## Two major charging networks for fleets
**Tesla Supercharger** is the largest fast-charging network in the US and offers broad national coverage, especially for long-distance travel and highway refueling. It is a strong candidate for fleets that need reliable DC fast charging on intercity routes.

**Electrify America** is another major nationwide network and one of the largest open DC fast-charging providers in the US. It has a significant footprint across highways and urban areas, making it a practical option for mixed-route fleet operations.

## Bottom line
For a sales fleet moving to EVs, the public charging landscape is large enough to support operations, but route planning still matters. Tesla Supercharger and Electrify America are two of the most important networks to factor into fleet charging strategy.
