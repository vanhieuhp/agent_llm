# Tesla Model Y vs. Ford Mustang Mach-E for a 100-Car Sales Fleet

## Quick comparison

| Category | Tesla Model Y | Ford Mustang Mach-E |
|---|---|---|
| EPA range | ~321–326 miles depending on trim | ~226–320 miles depending on trim |
| Seating | 5 | 5 |
| Cargo utility | ~29 cu ft behind rear seats; ~76 cu ft max | ~29.7 cu ft behind rear seats; ~59.7 cu ft max |
| Fast charging | Tesla Supercharger access; very fast DC charging | DC fast charging with access to Tesla Superchargers via NACS adapter |
| Fleet fit | Strong range, charging convenience, easy standardization | Strong practicality, good range on upper trims, broader dealer/service familiarity |

## Fleet assessment

**Tesla Model Y**
- Best strength is range plus charging convenience.
- Very efficient for high-mileage reps and long daily routes.
- Cargo space is solid for samples, bags, and day-trip gear.
- Main drawbacks are higher upfront cost and dependence on Tesla charging/service ecosystem.

**Ford Mustang Mach-E**
- Best strength is balanced crossover utility with strong available range.
- More traditional service and support model may be easier for fleet operations.
- Good fit for reps who want SUV-like packaging without sacrificing efficiency.
- Main drawback is lower maximum cargo space and more trim variation to manage.

## Recommendation

For a 100-car sales fleet, **the Tesla Model Y is the better default choice** because it offers the strongest combination of range, charging speed, and fleet-wide efficiency. If your team values a more traditional service network and wants a slightly more familiar mainstream fleet experience, the **Mach-E is the better backup option**.

**Recommendation: Choose the Tesla Model Y unless dealer/service simplicity is the top priority.**
