# Banoffee Pie

Source: BBC Good Food — *Banoffee pie recipe*

## Summary
A classic banoffee pie with a homemade sweet pastry shell, a layer of caramel (or dulce de leche), sliced bananas, whipped cream, and a little dark chocolate on top.

## Ingredients
- 4 bananas, sliced
- 394g caramel or dulce de leche
- 300ml double cream
- Dark chocolate, optional, for garnish
- **Pastry:** 100g butter, 200g plain flour, 1 medium egg (separated), 1 tbsp golden caster sugar

## Method
1. Make the pastry by pulsing butter and flour to breadcrumbs, then mix in egg yolk, sugar, and enough cold water to bring it together.
2. Knead gently, wrap, and chill for 30 minutes.
3. Roll out and line a greased 23cm loose-bottom tart tin.
4. Blind bake at 190C/170C fan/gas 4 for 15 minutes, then remove beans/paper, brush with egg white, and bake 15–20 minutes more until golden. Cool completely.
5. Spread half the caramel in the case, add sliced bananas, then top with the remaining caramel and chill.
6. Whip the cream until thick, spoon on top, and finish with grated dark chocolate.

## Notes
- Serves 8–10.
- Prep time: 30 mins; cook time: 30–35 mins.
- Best served fresh, but the pastry case can be made ahead.
