## 4) Major Challenges and Risks

Despite the positive scale and strategic positioning, epay operates in a demanding industry. The main challenges identified in the research are below.

### 1. Intense competition
The payments market is crowded and includes:

- card networks and processors
- digital wallets
- fintech distribution platforms
- retail payment aggregators
- payment orchestration providers
- alternative payment rails

This creates ongoing pressure on pricing, feature differentiation, and merchant retention. In such an environment, product innovation and ecosystem depth become critical.

### 2. Regulatory and compliance complexity
Payments companies face substantial obligations around:

- AML/KYC requirements
- consumer protection rules
- data privacy
- cybersecurity
- cross-border regulatory fragmentation
- merchant and partner compliance

A global network increases this complexity materially. As epay expands across markets and use cases, compliance capability becomes not just a support function, but a strategic differentiator.

### 3. Margin pressure in distribution-heavy channels
Where revenue depends on distribution or retail channel access, margins can be vulnerable to:

- fee compression
- dependence on retail partners
- shifting consumer behavior away from physical channels
- competitor undercutting

If the business remains too reliant on low-margin distribution, growth could translate into scale without proportional profitability improvement.

### 4. Technology and fraud risk
Payments infrastructure is exposed to:

- fraud and transaction abuse
- cyberattacks
- uptime and reliability issues
- settlement errors
- API and integration failures

As transaction volumes rise, so does the importance of real-time monitoring, resilient architecture, and effective risk controls.

### 5. Parent-level dependence
Because epay is part of Euronet, its trajectory is shaped by broader corporate priorities, capital allocation decisions, and performance across the group. That can be a strength, but it also means epay’s standalone strategic direction is influenced by enterprise-wide considerations.
